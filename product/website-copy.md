# GTM Brain website copy: selected source material

Source: existing website source for [gtm-brain.ai](https://gtm-brain.ai/), inspected 16 November 2026. This is a local website-source snapshot, not a verified live page capture. Selected text below is copied from the existing page. Older command names and roadmap language are omitted; use the product brief for this workshop.

## Positioning

> Make your company queryable. Inside Codex.

> An open-source company brain for GTM engineers. Bring customer knowledge, decisions and follow-ups into Codex. Ask questions across your existing tools. Build on what your company knows.

## Working with company knowledge

> Your customer history, calls, CRM records, strategy and decisions need to be available where the work happens.

> GTM Brain gives Codex that company context. Ask what changed, find the evidence behind a decision, or build the next workflow from what your team has already learned.

## Account brief

> Bring together prior conversations, people, decisions and live tool reads. See what is known, what changed and what you still need to ask.

## Existing stack

> Read from your connected CRM, email, calendar and notes. Each connection has its own access and permissions.

## Processing boundary

> Local storage describes where the brain files live. AI processing uses your chosen provider; connected services keep their own data policies.
