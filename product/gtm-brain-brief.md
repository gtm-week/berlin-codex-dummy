# The product we are distributing

## Real product context

GTM Brain is an open-source plugin for keeping GTM context and evidence across agent chats and projects. Its documentation names Codex as the primary host and Claude Code as beta. It helps retain the ICP, offer, customer evidence and the progress of repeatable work in local files. [Public documentation](https://github.com/gtm-week/gtm-brain)

The existing website emphasises bringing account history together, querying it and retaining useful decisions. [Included website copy](website-copy.md)

For these exercises, the useful capabilities are sourced account briefs, reusable working methods, standing company documents and retained decisions. Input quality, access and review still matter. The product does not replace a CRM, guarantee revenue or time savings, or make every source automatically available. Local files do not mean offline AI processing. [Documentation excerpt](public-documentation-excerpt.md) and [website copy](website-copy.md).

## Workshop scenario - fictional commercial offer

We help a team adopt this way of working through a scoped setup, training and handover. The sample price is EUR 2,400 before tax for one workflow, one team, two sessions and a documented handover. Some fictional opportunities contain different proposed scopes. These are teaching assumptions, not GTM Brain's published pricing or real offers.

Working audience: B2B SaaS teams, agencies and consultancies that repeatedly reconstruct account context from several sources. This is an ICP hypothesis for participants to improve from the sample calls, won/lost records and exclusions; it is not a confirmed company strategy.

## Claims to avoid

No measured customer results or testimonials are supplied. Do not present fictional conversations as real proof. Do not promise automatic sending, unrestricted client-data separation, a security certification, a supported integration or a multiplayer capability based on these sample files. Check current documentation for an actual product evaluation.

## Starting stack

For the exercise, the seller has a CRM export, recorder transcripts, email threads, website extracts, prospecting/enrichment tables and a content folder. These substitute for Attio/HubSpot, Granola/Pocket, Gmail, Context.dev, RowRun, BetterContact and shared documents. They are files; no account has been provisioned or connected.
