# Sample offer: one account-intelligence workflow

SYNTHETIC WORKSHOP DATA. All customer identities, conversations, activity and commercial terms are fictional.

Illustrative service terms, not official GTM Brain pricing. Version: 2026-11-16.

## Outcome

A team can turn permitted account evidence into a brief another colleague can inspect, then repeat the method on a second account.

## Included

- Scope one workflow and identify approved input sources with a named customer owner.
- Prepare five sample account briefs, reviewed with the team.
- Run two working sessions of 60 minutes each.
- Separate reusable instructions from account-specific evidence.
- Hand over the source map, instructions, quality checks and remaining questions.

## Acceptance

The brief identifies its sources, includes the latest known decision, preserves unknowns and does not copy one account's facts into another. A second colleague can review the result and repeat the method on a new account.

## Illustrative commercial terms

One-off fee: EUR 2,400 before tax. Provider subscriptions and third-party credits are excluded. Payment terms and start date are to be agreed; neither is assumed. No recurring software fee or continuing support is included. Larger or smaller scopes in the CRM are separate fictional proposals.

## Customer inputs and boundaries

The customer names an owner and approves the input material. Access setup depends on its host and services. No automated outreach, blanket production access, CRM migration or guaranteed financial/time-saving result is included. Additional work requires a separate scope.
