# Sample post: account preparation

SYNTHETIC WORKSHOP DATA. All customer identities, conversations, activity and commercial terms are fictional.

Workshop draft. Not published.

Before an account call, the difficult part is often finding the latest decision. The CRM contains a summary, the call note explains the original plan, and a later email changes the timing.

A useful account brief should show what changed and where the evidence lives. It should also leave a budget or buying role blank when nobody has confirmed it.

In this workshop's fictional Grovebridge example, the call mentions a November pilot. A later email moves the earliest possible start to January. Summarising the call accurately would still produce the wrong preparation if we ignored that email.

Start with one account, read its sources in date order, and ask a colleague to check the brief before turning the method into a reusable skill.
