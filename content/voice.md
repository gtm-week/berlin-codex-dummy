# Writing guidance for the workshop

Adapted from the draft GTM Tech Week Voice guide. This file governs the sample exercise only.

- Start with the reader's concrete work and why the information matters.
- Explain how the proposed task helps; avoid generic promises about AI or growth.
- Distinguish source facts, hypotheses, proposals and confirmed decisions.
- Keep customer language when quoting. Mark all sample quotes as fictional.
- Make a follow-up easy to answer, with one relevant next action.
- Do not treat email discovery as qualification or enthusiasm as buying authority.
- Do not invent results, discounts, certifications, testimonials or a budget.
- Use short connected paragraphs and plain hyphens. No slogans or decorative contrasts.

The two posts and outreach example are sample drafts, not previously published material or evidence of marketing performance.
