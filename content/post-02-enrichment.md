# Sample post: after enrichment

SYNTHETIC WORKSHOP DATA. All customer identities, conversations, activity and commercial terms are fictional.

Workshop draft. Not published.

A matched contact record gives you a way to identify someone. It does not tell you whether they can approve a purchase or whether the account needs your help.

Our fictional Brindle example makes the difference visible. Jonas can build the workflow and is excited about it. Iris owns the delivery standard and the budget. A message asking Jonas to sign would skip the actual decision process.

Use enrichment alongside the conversation history. Name the problem the team described, show which evidence supports your interpretation, and ask for the missing decision-maker when needed.

The next useful action may be a scope review with another stakeholder, rather than another row added to an outreach list.
